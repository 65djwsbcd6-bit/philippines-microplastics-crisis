# Photo and video shot list

Everything goes in folders next to `index.html`:

```
index.html
PHOTOS.md
images/   <- all photos (JPG) + CREDITS.txt
videos/   <- hero-manila-bay.mp4
```

Until a file exists, the page shows a labelled placeholder (photos) or the hero photo / dark background (video).

## Rules
- Only use photos and videos you have the right to use: your own, or licensed (for example Creative Commons on Wikimedia Commons). Keep the source and licence for each in `images/CREDITS.txt`.
- Only use a photo if you can confirm it was taken in the place it represents (a Manila Bay slot needs a real Manila Bay photo).
- Names must match exactly: lowercase, hyphens, `.jpg`.
- Photos: JPG, about 1600 px wide, ideally under 500 KB each. GitHub's web upload limit is 25 MB per file.

Philippine sources: Wikimedia Commons, Greenpeace Philippines, EcoWaste Coalition, DENR, PRRC, MMDA, UP Marine Science Institute, or your own photos and footage.

## Home background video

| Done | File | Must show |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] | `videos/hero-manila-bay.mp4` | A calm 10 to 20 second clip of the Manila Bay shoreline or bayfront with plastic waste visible (waves, tide or slow camera movement) |

Video specs:
- Format: MP4 (H.264). No audio needed, it plays muted.
- Size: 1280x720 or 1920x1080, under 10 MB (GitHub's web upload limit is 25 MB). Shorten or compress it with a free tool such as HandBrake if it is bigger.
- Length: 10 to 20 seconds, ideally one that loops without a visible jump.
- It plays automatically, muted and looping, with a Pause button. It stays still if the visitor's device asks for reduced motion.
- Choose a calm, not-too-bright clip: white text sits on top of it.
- Put the source and licence of the video in `images/CREDITS.txt`.
